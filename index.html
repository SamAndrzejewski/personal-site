Side Quests

Side Quest · 01

Create a fishing videogame: Brackish

Brackish is a fishing game with one non-negotiable requirement: it has to be genuinely, grin-inducingly fun. Not "fun for an indie game" — fun, period. The tagline tells you the rest: your boat is smaller than you think.

Under the calm surface it's a full-stack creative challenge — game feel, the physics of the cast and the fight, the "one more catch" loop, and the creeping suspicion that something much bigger is circling below. Building a world people want to stay in is its own kind of engineering.

Side Quest · 02

Set a Guinness World Record

Most consecutive L-sit pull-ups: 30. Set on May 30, 2025 in St. Simons Island, Georgia.

An L-sit pull-up makes you hold your legs parallel to the floor for every single rep. I saw that this record was relatively low hanging fruit and spent about a month training for it. Hopefully it doesn't get broken too soon...

Side Quest · 04

Memorize 5,000 constants & facts

The mass of the Moon (7.3 × 10²² kg — about 1/81 of Earth). The energy density of diesel. The GDP of Indonesia. I keep a running arsenal of ~5,000 constants and facts, drilled daily with spaced repetition — the raw material for Fermi estimation.

With enough anchors in your head, you can sanity-check almost any claim, size almost any market, and estimate almost any quantity to within an order of magnitude — no search bar required.
